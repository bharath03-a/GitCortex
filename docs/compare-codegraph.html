Compare · v0.8.0 vs CodeGraph 1.5.0

How GitCortex v0.8.0 compares to CodeGraph 1.5.0.

We indexed five large real-world repositories (tokio, django, next.js, moby, spring-boot) with both GitCortex v0.8.0 and CodeGraph 1.5.0, then ran all 10 search & caller tasks through Codex CLI (gpt-6.1-sol, low reasoning). Dated historical 3-client v0.7.6 results are preserved below.

In short

GitCortex v0.8.0 achieved 10/10 accuracy (1.000), the lowest uncached tokens (125.2k, −23.5% vs baseline), 4.1× faster aggregate indexing (50.9 s vs 206.2 s), and 52% smaller total disk (721 MiB vs 1,501 MiB) — including 14.8× faster indexing on next.js.

CodeGraph 1.5.0 scored 9/10 (0.900), still missing nextjs-callers-normalize-page-path (0.00), used slightly fewer total (cached + uncached) tokens (626k vs 653k), and beat GitCortex cleanly on moby indexing speed and size. See where CodeGraph wins.

Cold full-index speed & disk footprint (5 large repos)

Aggregate cold indexing across all 5 big repos is 4.1× faster and 52% smaller on GitCortex v0.8.0 — driven by huge wins on next.js (14.8× faster, 78% smaller) and spring-boot (3.1× faster, 73% smaller).

Where CodeGraph wins

  • moby indexing time & disk: CodeGraph indexed moby in 6.68 s / 109.6 MiB vs GitCortex's 23.84 s / 297.7 MiB because CodeGraph skips vendored Go directories (36,837 nodes / 120,384 edges vs GitCortex's 269,433 nodes / 1,072,083 edges).
  • Total (cached + uncached) tokens on Codex: CodeGraph used 626,035 total tokens vs GitCortex's 653,151 (−4.2% total), and used fewer uncached tokens on 7 of the 10 individual tasks (GitCortex won aggregate uncached tokens 125.2k vs 129.7k due to large savings on django-callers-filter and both next.js tasks).
